Everything Is Down

The world is down, not around!

When we think about where the rest of the world is, we think of places on a map, or which direction we'd travel to get there. We never think about where things actually are, relative to us, right now. Mostly, they're beneath your feet, somewhere you could point to directly, through thousands of miles of earth.
