Antipode

The Earth, made transparent. Look straight down and you're looking at your antipode — the far side of the world, upside-down beneath your shoes. Tilt away from it and you sweep across the rest of the planet, out to the horizon, which is your own position in every direction at once. These apps draw what you'd see, live, locked to the world as you move.
