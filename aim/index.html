Point Through the Earth — batch + map

Facing north at your location, this lists which way to face and how far to tip your arm down to point — through the planet — at each city, and plots them on a map flipped east–west, the way the far side actually looks when you stare down through the Earth.

Math: from straight-down (your antipode) swing toward the target by half its angular distance from the antipode. Depression below horizon = Δ/2; azimuth = great-circle initial bearing. Map is mirrored east–west to match the through-the-Earth view. Geocoding via Open-Meteo, coastlines via Natural Earth.
