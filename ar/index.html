ANTIPODE

See straight through the planet. Point your phone at the ground and the far side of the Earth is drawn on the camera image, as if the planet were transparent.

Straight down is your exact antipode. Tilting toward the horizon reveals coastlines closer to home; the horizon itself is your own position, in every direction at once.

Needs motion & orientation, location and camera permission. Everything runs on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
No sensors (desktop)? Drag to look around. Scroll to zoom.
